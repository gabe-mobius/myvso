# MyVSO: Rating criteria for common conditions

Text from 38 CFR Part 4 via the eCFR API (https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-10-21/title-38.xml?part=4&section=...), eCFR up to date as of 2026-10-21. Rating levels are quoted from the regulation; "/" replaces "|". The "Ask about" column lists only the devices, treatments, and medical events named in that row's rule text, with no thresholds; the assistant asks each as its own yes/no fact question (E5 step 1) and never says what an answer means. The assistant shows the veteran only the "What VA looks at" column, in plain words, never the levels or percentages (ASSISTANT_INSTRUCTIONS.md E5 step 3); the levels stay here for maintainers and the checks. For any condition not listed, look it up live at https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/chapter-I/part-4 and give the link and date checked.
