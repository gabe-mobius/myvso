# MyVSO: VA facts the assistant may rely on

Apart from RATINGS.md, FORMS.md, and the live lookups the instructions allow (E5, J3, O2), the assistant may state VA facts only from this file, and only rows marked `sourced`. Every `sourced` row links to an official page, quotes the words that support it, and gives the date the page was checked. A row marked `UNVERIFIED` has not been confirmed on an allowed official page. Treat it as something to look into, not as a fact. The assistant must not present an `UNVERIFIED` row as fact.

Run `python3 check_va_facts.py` after any edit.

## Combined ratings math

| # | Topic | Fact | Source URL | Source quote or value | Date checked | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CR1 | Efficiency method | Ratings combine by remaining efficiency, not by adding. 60% and 30% combine to 72%. | https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/chapter-I/part-4/subpart-A/section-4.25 | 38 CFR 4.25: "a person having a 60 percent disability is considered 40 percent efficient ... The individual is thus 72 percent disabled" | 2026-10-14 | sourced |
| CR2 | Rounding | The combined value is rounded to the nearest 10; values ending in 5 round up. | https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/chapter-I/part-4/subpart-A/section-4.25 | "nearest number divisible by 10, and combined values ending in 5 will be adjusted upward. Thus, with a 50 percent disability and a 30 percent disability, the combined value will be found to be 65 percent, but the 65 percent must be converted to 70 percent" | 2026-10-14 | sourced |
| CR3 | Bilateral factor | For both arms, both legs, or paired skeletal muscles, combine the two sides, then add 10% of that value. | https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/chapter-I/part-4/subpart-A/section-4.26 | 38 CFR 4.26: "the ratings for the disabilities of the right and left sides will be combined as usual, and 10 percent of this value will be added (i.e., not combined)"; worked example "60, 21 and 20 ... converted to 70 percent" | 2026-10-14 | sourced |
